# NAZA Road Scanner Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 26, 2026

NAZA Road Scanner (“NAZA,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a scanner-only mobile application that runs its language model on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles.

## Information we collect

NAZA does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise device location, contacts, photos, audio, or advertising identifier.

When you enter a road or destination and run a scan, that text is processed locally by the on-device language model. It is not sent to NAZA, an AI API, or a cloud inference service.

If you choose to save a scan, NAZA stores the entered location, scan result, timestamp, model name, generator mode, and related scan details locally on your device. Scan history is off by default. Local app settings, including your privacy and security preferences, are also stored on the device.

## How information is used

Locally stored information is used only to provide app features such as running scans, displaying results, searching scan history, and applying your settings. NAZA does not sell, rent, or use your scan information for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

## Model downloads and third-party services

On first setup, NAZA downloads a model file from one of its configured HTTPS model hosts. The app may connect to Hugging Face, GitHub Releases, or Pinata/IPFS, and may use a custom Pinata-compatible mirror entered by you in Model Manager. These services may receive normal network information such as your IP address, request time, device/network metadata, and User-Agent according to their own privacy policies. NAZA does not send your scan text or scan history as part of the model download.

Please review the privacy policies of any model host you use:

- [Hugging Face Privacy Policy](https://huggingface.co/privacy)
- [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement)
- [Pinata Privacy Policy](https://www.pinata.cloud/privacy)

NAZA does not include third-party advertising, analytics, crash reporting, social sign-in, or cloud synchronization.

## Data security

Saved scan history, settings, and downloaded model files are stored locally. NAZA encrypts the stored history and downloaded model using encryption keys maintained on the device. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and NAZA cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data retention and deletion

NAZA retains locally saved information until you delete it using the app’s available history/settings controls or uninstall the app. Uninstalling normally removes the app’s local data according to your operating system. Model files and temporary download files can be removed through Model Manager or by uninstalling the app.

Because NAZA does not maintain a server account or receive scan data, there is no NAZA-hosted scan database to access or delete.

## Children’s privacy

NAZA is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the situation.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app’s data practices change. The updated version will be posted with a new effective date.
